Privacy Policy

NET JRF PATH · Last updated: 22 August 2026

NET JRF PATH is an educational learning and preparation platform, operated as an individual project, designed to help learners prepare for the UGC NET and JRF examinations through practice questions, quizzes, learning activities and progress tracking.

This Privacy Policy explains what information NET JRF PATH may collect, why it is collected, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use NET JRF PATH, we may collect the following information:

Account information

  • Your name associated with your account.
  • Your email address.
  • Your Google account identifier when you sign in using Google.

Learning and assessment information

When you use the platform, we may record information necessary to provide learning and progress features, including:

  • Questions attempted.
  • Answers submitted.
  • Correct and incorrect responses.
  • Scores and accuracy.
  • Time spent on learning activities.
  • Quests or learning activities completed.
  • Other progress information generated through your use of the platform.

Technical and analytics information

We use Google Analytics to understand how the platform is used and to improve it. Google Analytics may collect information such as your approximate location (derived from IP address), browser and device type, pages visited, and general usage patterns. This information is aggregated and is not used to identify you individually. You can learn more about how Google handles this data in Google's Privacy & Terms .

Basic technical information (such as IP address) may also be processed by the infrastructure providers used to operate the platform, including our hosting and content-delivery provider, as necessary for security, performance and delivery of the service.

2. Google Sign-In

NET JRF PATH may allow you to sign in using your Google Account. When you choose Google Sign-In, Google provides NET JRF PATH with basic account information necessary to authenticate you — your name, email address and a unique account identifier. NET JRF PATH requests only this basic profile information and does not request access to your Google Drive, Gmail, contacts, files, or any other Google service.

We use this information to:

  • Authenticate your identity.
  • Create and maintain your NET JRF PATH account.
  • Associate your learning progress with your account.
  • Help protect the platform from unauthorised access.
We do not use Google account information to sell advertising or to sell your personal information.

3. How We Use Information

Information collected through NET JRF PATH may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain the learning platform.
  • Authenticate users.
  • Save and display learning progress.
  • Calculate scores, accuracy and other learning statistics.
  • Provide personalised or progress-based learning features.
  • Understand aggregate usage patterns through analytics.
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the platform.
  • Identify and resolve technical problems.
  • Improve the educational experience and functionality of the platform.

4. How We Store Information

NET JRF PATH uses third-party infrastructure and services to provide authentication, database, hosting and content-delivery functionality.

User account and learning information may therefore be processed and stored using these service providers on behalf of NET JRF PATH.

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Sharing of Information

NET JRF PATH does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising, marketing, profiling, or commercial purposes.

NET JRF PATH does not use advertising trackers or third-party advertising networks.

Google, Supabase, GitHub Pages and Cloudflare are used as technical service providers for authentication, secure data storage, hosting and content delivery. Information processed through these services is used solely to provide NET JRF PATH's functionality and is not provided to third parties for advertising or commercial purposes.

NET JRF PATH does not disclose personal information to third parties except where disclosure is required by applicable law or necessary to protect the security of the service and its users.

6. Learning and Performance Data

NET JRF PATH is designed as an educational platform. Information about your attempts, scores, accuracy and completed activities is used to provide progress reports and learning statistics to you within your own account. This information is not shown publicly or to other users.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

NET JRF PATH may use browser storage technologies, including cookies or local storage, where necessary to maintain sessions, remember preferences, support authentication, or enable analytics (see Section 1, "Technical and analytics information").

You can control cookies and browser storage through your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics using tools such as the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on . Disabling certain technologies may affect some functionality of the platform.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain learning records, meet legitimate operational requirements, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required for these purposes, we may delete or anonymise it, subject to technical and legal requirements.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the ability to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

NET JRF PATH does not currently offer a self-service option to delete your account from within the app. To request deletion of your account or personal information, please email us at the address in Section 14 and we will process your request.

Where processing is based on consent, applicable law may provide you with the right to withdraw that consent. Withdrawal of consent does not affect processing that was lawfully carried out before withdrawal.

10. Children's Privacy

NET JRF PATH is intended for learners preparing for UGC NET/JRF and other postgraduate-level competitive examinations, and is generally used by adult learners. We do not knowingly design the service to collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is prohibited by applicable law. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

11. Third-Party Services

NET JRF PATH relies on third-party services for functions such as authentication, hosting, database storage, content delivery and analytics. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

The principal services used by the platform are:

  • Google — Sign-In authentication, and Google Analytics for usage analytics.
  • Supabase — authentication, database and related infrastructure.
  • GitHub Pages — hosting/delivery of the web application.
  • Cloudflare — content delivery and network security.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information. Authentication and access controls are used where appropriate to restrict access to user information.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their own Google Account and devices used to access NET JRF PATH.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to NET JRF PATH, applicable law, technology or our data practices.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page together with a revised "Last updated" date.
